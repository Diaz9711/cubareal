October 1, 2026

Homeland and Life.

Young, and not afraid, knowing they can die for their freedom and for Cuba.

Not a party. Anna Sofía Benítez Silvente and her mother, Caridad Silvente, were both sentenced. The pastor did not go alone: his wife and two other people were taken on the way. The two from the little room are still in a Holguín prison.

Open the case

Anna and Caridad

Anna Sofía Benítez Silvente and Caridad Silvente, mother and daughter

The summons was in the mother’s name. Both filmed the officer at the door in Alamar. Both were found guilty. Two years of limitation of liberty each. The officer’s lawyer had asked for prison for Anna. The court did not impose it. Their lawyer, Roberto Ortega, said the written ruling comes in about 15 days.

Open the case

Pregonero de Cristo

Rolando Fidel Pérez Lora, his wife Gelayne Rodríguez Ávila, David Espinosa and Espinosa’s wife

He was going to Cojímar to stand with them. State Security threw him to the ground and handcuffed him. On camera he asked why. An officer said it would be written up at the station. CubaNet reported that Gelayne, Baptist leader David Espinosa and Espinosa’s wife were detained too.

Open the case

El4tico

Ernesto Ricardo Medina and Kamil Zayas Pérez, Holguín

Taken from their homes in Piedra Blanca on February 6. On October 2, 2026 ADN Cuba reported the Holguín prosecutor asked for eight years for Medina and ten for Zayas, and the court opened the trial. They are still in Holguín Provincial Prison.

They did not all get taken. CubaNet named sister Nadieska Almeida, priest Lester Rafael Zayas Díaz and Pedro Álvarez, a July 11 prisoner, among those who reached the court in Cojímar.

Blackouts

Whole days in the dark

In August 2026 generation covered less than a third of demand (OCHA). On September 18 MINEM confirmed the year's eighth nationwide blackout. It is not a short outage: it is rebuilding life around two or three hours, when they come.

Health

Empty pharmacies, collapsing hospitals

The government admits only about 30% of the basic drug list is available. More than 100,000 surgeries waiting — about 12,000 of them children. At Ramón González Coro Hospital they have operated by phone flashlight. The UN warns 70,000 children are at risk of missing vaccines.

Water

No power, no water

More than 3.1 million people face total or partial water cuts. The state admits more than 40% of pumped water is lost in broken pipes. In Havana, 40 to 70%. 87% of supply depends on the grid: no power, no tap.

Independent

8 in 10

skipped a meal · OCDH 2026

How this is counted

Ninth study of the Cuban Observatory of Human Rights. 1,318 interviews, June and July 2026. Eight in ten said they skipped a meal. It is a survey, not a census. Whoever could not answer is not in it.

Independent

94%

live in extreme poverty · OCDH

How this is counted

The same OCDH ninth study, June–July 2026. The 94% fall under the extreme-poverty line the Observatory uses, not the state’s. ONEI does not publish this figure. It can run low or high depending on who answered.

Independent

1,355

on the public list · PD noviembre

How this is counted

Prisoners Defenders, list closed in August 2026: 1,339 verified political prisoners. That month, 19 added and 7 removed. It is not a Cuban court figure. PD says it may not capture every case. “Pre-criminal dangerousness” files are separate: PD estimates at least 11,000, and they are not inside the 1,339. Public list, read when this page opens: 1,355 names, noviembre 2026. 19 new this month. The August 2026 report still closed at 1,339.

Official

9.43 M

inhabitants · ONEI, end of 2025

How this is counted

ONEI, Demographic Indicators 2025: 9,434,593 residents on December 31, 2025. It is the state’s figure. Foreign Affairs puts the exodus since 2020 at 13 to 24% of the population. If departures were not fully registered, the official number stays high.

Why the power fails

Fuel, old plants, no reserve. On September 18 the grid fell for the eighth time this year.

Why the medicine is missing

The government itself says only 30% of the basic drug list is available.

Why the peso does not buy

The state still books one dollar at 24 pesos. On the street it is above 700.

Why people leave

Since 2020, between 13 and 24% of the population has left. Not for no reason.
