Where each number comes from

Who counted, between which dates, and what the count may be missing.

DocumentCR-07
RevisionNov 25, 2026
Reading1 min
Linked sources4

Official · A figure the state publishes. It is cited, and its limit is said.

Independent · An NGO or an archive. Limited access to the island. What it may miss is said.

Independent

8,267 Deaths and disappearances

Cuba Archive, report “The Human Cost of Raúl Castro’s Rule,” March 30, 2026. It counts deaths and disappearances for political reasons from January 1, 1959 to March 15, 2026. Inside that: 3,084 firing-squad executions and 805 deaths in 1959. The archive says the real number is higher: only what it could document is in. It is not a state list.

Source

Independent

1,339 Political prisoners

Prisoners Defenders, close of August 2026. People verified as political prisoners. That month: 19 added, 7 removed. Since July 2021 the running total is 2,167. PD works from outside and says it may not capture every case. The at least 11,000 pre-criminal files are not in this number.

Source

Independent

94% Extreme poverty

OCDH, ninth study, 1,318 interviews between June and July 2026. The 94% fall under the Observatory’s extreme-poverty line. Eight in ten said they skipped a meal. It is not the census. Whoever was not interviewed is not in it.

Source

Official

9.43 M Population

ONEI, Demographic Indicators 2025: 9,434,593 residents on December 31, 2025. It is the figure the state publishes. Foreign Affairs estimates that since 2020 between 13 and 24% of the population left. If that exit was not fully recorded, 9.43 million sits above the number of people living on the island today.
