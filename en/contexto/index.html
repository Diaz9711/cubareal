Why the country is falling

Power, medicine, the peso and the exit. Each block states the cause and the source.

DocumentCR-08
RevisionNov 25, 2026
Reading1 min

Documented

The power

On September 18, 2026 UNE and the energy ministry recorded the eighth nationwide blackout of the year. It is not a one-day failure. The system falls because fuel is short, the plants are old and maintenance does not keep up: when one unit drops, there is no reserve to cover it. On September 22 the electric company had 105 Havana circuits out and a gap of 336 MW. 87% of the water depends on that same grid. No power, no tap.

Official

The medicine

Díaz-Canel and MINSAP have said only 30% of the basic drug list is available: seven in ten essential medicines are missing. About 100,000 surgeries are waiting, close to 12,000 of them children (Al Jazeera). Infant mortality went from 4.0 in 2018 to 9.9 in 2025. It is not one missing bottle. It is the system that was supposed to buy it, make it and get it to the hospital.

Independent

The peso

The state still books one dollar at 24 pesos for its companies. For people the Central Bank publishes another rate, and on the street elTOQUE is above 700. The wage is paid in pesos and the food that shows up is priced in dollars. That gap is the inflation that eats the month. OCDH’s 94% in extreme poverty is not a mystery next to that rate.

Documented

The exit

Foreign Affairs puts the exodus since 2020 at 13 to 24% of the population, mostly the young. ONEI closed 2025 at 9.43 million residents. People do not leave for no reason. They leave the power that does not come back, the hospital without medicine, the peso that buys nothing, and prison for asking for all three. How it got here is in History of Cuba, from 1959.
